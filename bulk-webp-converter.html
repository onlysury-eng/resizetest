Why convert your images to WebP?

WebP is a modern image format developed by Google that provides superior lossless and lossy compression for images on the web. Using WebP enables webmasters and developers to create smaller, richer images that make the web faster.

Vs. PNG (Lossless)

WebP lossless images are typically 26% smaller in size compared to PNGs, while retaining exact transparency and identical visual data.

Vs. JPEG (Lossy)

WebP lossy images are 25-34% smaller than comparable JPEG images at equivalent SSIM quality indexes.
