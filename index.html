# 100% Privacy & Client-Side Security

Unlike traditional online converters that upload your personal photos, sensitive graphics, or work assets to a remote server database, PixelShrink operates entirely inside your web browser.

Using advanced HTML5 Canvas and local web worker memory APIs, your files are processed locally on your machine's hardware. Your images never touch our servers, ensuring absolute confidentiality and lightning-fast batch speeds without network latency.
