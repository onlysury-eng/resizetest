How to compress multiple JPGs without losing quality

Raw photographs and unoptimized web assets often carry massive file sizes due to ultra-high resolutions and hidden metadata. Serving these uncompressed files on a website drastically increases page load times, hurts your Core Web Vitals, and damages your Google search rankings.

The 70% Quality Sweet Spot

When compressing JPEGs for web delivery, you rarely need to save files at 100% quality. Human eyes generally cannot detect the difference between a 100% quality JPEG and a 70% quality JPEG, but the file size difference is massive.

Our bulk compressor defaults to a 70% fidelity setting. This strips out invisible data and unnecessary EXIF metadata (like camera settings and GPS coordinates) while maintaining a crisp, sharp image ready for web publication.

Unlike standard cloud compressors that force you to upload your photography to their servers, BulkImgResize does all the math locally on your machine. You get enterprise-grade batch compression with absolute privacy and zero upload waiting times.
